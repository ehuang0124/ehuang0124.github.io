Research & coursework

  • AI evaluation & human preference (LMArena)

    Independent Study in CS · Cornell Tech

    AI evaluation · Pairwise human preference · NIST AI RMF · Empirical analysis

    Examines how crowd-sourced pairwise preferences on LMArena evaluate open-ended language model behavior, and where arena voting aligns with or falls short of trustworthiness criteria under the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (criterion coverage, validity, and reliability). Includes large-scale screening of 137,293 real-world conversations from the French public compar:IA arena corpus—isolating 8,739 civic and political prompts—to study how raters judge models on contested public-interest topics. Repository

  • Tech, democracy & media

    Graduate Seminar · Cornell Tech

    Electoral institutions · Surveillance & privacy · Civil liberties · Socio-technical analysis

    Two institutional case studies on how technical architecture and public governance jointly shape democratic legitimacy:

    • Electronic voting machines — Analyzed two decades of U.S. direct-recording electronic (DRE) voting systems, Help America Vote Act certification gaps, disability accessibility mandates, and voter-verified paper audit trails—showing how security, usability, and unequal precinct administration mediate public trust.
    • Taiwan's COVID-19 response — Examined digital contact tracing, electronic quarantine enforcement, and state–civic data integration during the pandemic, evaluating privacy, surveillance, and civil-liberties tradeoffs alongside democratic accountability.

  • AI-assisted trade skills for workforce development (BenchMate)

    HCI & Product Studio Capstone · Cornell Tech

    Human-computer interaction · Contextual inquiry · Multimodal AI · Workforce development

    Conducted qualitative interviews, contextual inquiries, and apprenticeship ecosystem research (grounded in U.S. Department of Labor and BLS data) to study barriers in hands-on technical learning. Co-built BenchMate, an instructor-in-the-loop system that converts shop demonstrations, equipment manuals, and safety SOPs into step-by-step multimodal guides with page-cited safety checks—expanding training capacity for makerspaces, community colleges, and reentry workforce programs. Repository

  • Recruitment fraud labeler on Bluesky

    Trust & Safety · Cornell Tech

    Platform governance · Composable moderation · Bluesky · Scam defense

    Built a Bluesky moderation labeler to surface recruitment fraud, impersonated employers, and deceptive employment schemes targeting job seekers—exploring how user-subscribed, decentralized labeling separates safety signaling from centralized takedowns while managing false-positive risk.

  • Internet law

    Graduate Coursework · Cornell Tech

    First Amendment · Section 230 & EU DSA · Copyright & fair use · Digital privacy

    Doctrinal study of platform governance and speech regulation across U.S. and European legal frameworks—covering First Amendment scrutiny, intermediary liability under CDA Section 230 and the EU Digital Services Act, generative AI defamation and authorship, DMCA §512 safe harbors and fair use, Fourth Amendment surveillance limits, and anonymous online speech.
