Eva Huang

I work at the intersection of technology and public life, drawing on computer science and political science to explore how AI systems can better reflect human preferences and democratic values.

My research focuses on AI evaluation and public interest technology. I combine computational analysis with interviews and institutional study, asking what we measure, whose perspectives count, and how technical choices shape everyday life.

Previously, I built cloud infrastructure at Salesforce. Beyond engineering, I have worked on education access and mentorship for Taiwanese students, helping people find their way across unfamiliar institutions and possibilities.

I also write essays about migration, cities, and belonging. My collection, 《太陽雨》 (Sunny Rain), follows life between Taiwan and the United States. A second manuscript, 《經過的方式》 (Ways of Passing By), is in progress.

Across research and writing, I return to questions of trust, place, and how we live together in community.
