Eva Huang

I build technology for public life. What drives me is civic tech: making public services, public information, and civic participation work for the people they are meant to serve.

My work sits where policy meets product. I like turning rules, constraints, and community needs into tools people can actually use, and asking who a system is built for and who it leaves out.

I started out building cloud infrastructure at Salesforce, then joined an early-stage startup, where I learned to ship quickly, stay close to users, and own a product end to end.

Outside of my day job, I have spent six years in nonprofit work on higher education access and equity, mentoring Taiwanese students and helping people find their way through unfamiliar institutions. I was on the founding team and executive board of Beyond Taiwan.

Across all of it, I keep coming back to one goal: public systems that are fair, legible, and easy to use.
