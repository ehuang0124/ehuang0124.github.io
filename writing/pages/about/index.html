About Me

Eva Huang （黃樂恩） is a creative non-fiction writer. Born and raised in Taiwan, she left to pursue a Computer Science degree in Atlanta, GA. In 2022, she published her book《太陽雨》"Sunny Rain", a collection of essays exploring early memories, sense of place, and coming-of-age experiences. A second book revolving around roadtrip, landscape and culture 《經過的方式》"Ways of Passing By" is tentatively scheduled to publish in 2023. Eva also contributes to online columns for social & cultural critiques. She currently resides in Bay Area, CA, where she works as a software engineer.

1998年生，寫散文、詩和代碼，中英輸入法皆可。北一人社、GT資工，旅居舊金山灣區，公路旅行五萬英里逾。著有隨筆散文《太陽雨》（島座放送，2022）。
