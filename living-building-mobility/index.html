Living Building Initiative

Map access.
Design belonging.

A sustainable building succeeds only when people can reach it. This research combined four mobility personas, user interviews, and GIS to place community access inside the design conversation.

Research approach

From human needs to spatial evidence.

The work connected qualitative insight with geographic context. That combination makes access visible—not as an abstract radius, but as a lived journey through a network of useful places.

Listen

Use interviews to understand mobility expectations, constraints, and day-to-day experience.

Frame

Build four personas that preserve different needs instead of averaging people into one user.

Map

Plot destinations and resources to show the broader ecosystem around the building.

Connect

Bring human and spatial evidence together to support clearer design conversations.

What this view makes possible.

  • Align stakeholders around the real geography of access.
  • Expose gaps between a destination and the people it intends to serve.
  • Ground sustainable-place decisions in both research and spatial context.
