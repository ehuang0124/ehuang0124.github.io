Japan administrative shapes in personal/travel/map/jp-admin.js

Source geometry: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (国土地理院),
Global Map Japan, 2016 edition (地球地図日本).
Converted to TopoJSON and published by dataofjapan/land:
https://github.com/dataofjapan/land

The atlas simplifies those prefecture polygons and projects them into the
page's Natural Earth 1 coordinate system. The drawing illustrates the 47
prefectures (都道府縣) and the nine conventional regions (北海道、東北、關東、
中部、近畿、中國、四國、九州、沖繩). Okinawa is kept separate from Kyushu,
matching the source's region table; the statutory eight-region system
sometimes folds Okinawa into Kyushu. These shapes are not surveyed
boundaries and not a legal map.

Global Map Japan asks non-commercial reuse to name the source (地球地図日本).
That credit is this file and the provenance note in map/README.md. The public
page does not carry a data-source line.
