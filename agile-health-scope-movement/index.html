    Leadership takeaway

    Reliability/failover/safety and onboarding/environment enablement together account for 53.6% of feature/customer movement. The bucket is not one kind of interruption.

    Method and interpretation

    Counts use movement occurrences from the end-of-day day-one sprint baseline. The same WI can contribute more than once when it moves repeatedly.

    Important evidence boundary

    The structured reason field was blank for every WI. “Priority shift / uncoded reason” is a residual 28-event group after stronger bug, feature/customer, and blocker signals were excluded—not a recorded explanation. Customer identities were also omitted because the structured customer field was blank.
